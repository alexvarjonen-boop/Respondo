# Respondo AI — International organic launch brief (12 Oct 2026)

## Accurate international positioning
**Brand:** Respondo AI, a Finnish B2B website customer service chatbot.
**Canonical domain:** https://respondoai.fi/
**Main use case:** Automated answers to visitor questions using company-approved information; capture contact/booking enquiries and enable human follow-up when needed.
**Languages presented:** Finnish, Swedish and English.
**Commercial availability:** Paid plans are currently available **only to Finnish businesses**. Do NOT imply international checkout is live.
**Key differentiator to demonstrate, not promise blindly:** Answers are grounded in the individual customer's own provided or approved knowledge and do not need a generic hallucinated reply.

## One-paragraph directory listing (EN)
Respondo AI is a Finnish web-based customer service bot for companies that receive recurring questions through their websites. Businesses can provide approved answers and website content so visitors can ask about services, products, prices, hours, deliveries and appointments. The platform supports contact and booking requests, employee handoff and Finnish, Swedish and English conversations. Paid business subscriptions are currently available only in Finland. Learn more and test the public demo at https://respondoai.fi/.

## Thought-leadership angles worth pitching
- Why reliable AI support must know when not to answer.
- The overlooked problem of company-specific knowledge isolation in chatbots.
- Why home services and car repair workshops need customer FAQ bots as much as ecommerce stores do.
- A practical checklist for avoiding unsupported claims about pricing and service availability.

## Organic launch checklist — not yet published
1. Ensure English resource guides and XML sitemap are indexed in Search Console.
2. Post a genuinely informative LinkedIn article linking to one guide, not a generic promotional announcement.
3. Submit a truthful product profile to 2–3 industry directories only where editorial criteria fit; use canonical .fi domain.
4. Seek independent links through Finnish/European ecommerce and small business communities. Never purchase spammy backlinks.
5. Track Search Console impressions, countries, nonbranded queries, top landing pages and qualified demo enquiries weekly.
6. Revisit naming differentiation before large international campaigns: unrelated sites use similar Respondo branding (respondo.ai and respondo.fi). Check trademark and domain availability through counsel rather than assuming exclusive rights.
