## Launch objective
Acquire the first 100 paying European B2B customers with zero paid-ad spend, then scale the channels that convert.

## Wave 1 markets
1. Finland — Finnish
2. Sweden — Swedish
3. Netherlands — English
4. Ireland — English
5. Norway — English

Germany, Denmark and additional EU markets are held for a later country-specific compliance/localization pass before cold email.

## Best initial verticals
- Cleaning/home services
- Moving/logistics
- HVAC/plumbing/electrical
- Auto repair/service
- Beauty/wellness/appointment businesses
- Small ecommerce stores with repetitive delivery/return/product questions

## Offer
- Personalized website bot demo using the prospect's own public website information.
- 3-day free trial.
- Starter €29.90/mo, Advanced €39.90/mo, Business €49.90/mo.
- Annual billing has no discount and costs the equivalent of twelve monthly payments:
  - Starter €29.90/mo billed €358.80/year
  - Advanced €39.90/mo billed €478.80/year
  - Business €49.90/mo billed €598.80/year

## Outreach rules
- Prefer public company-role addresses such as info@, sales@, hello@ or contact forms.
- Never send to a private/personal address unless it is clearly published for business enquiries.
- Every cold email must identify Respondo and contain a simple opt-out sentence.
- No purchased email lists.
- No deceptive subject lines or fake prior-conversation wording.
- Keep each country in a separate batch so reply and conversion rates are measurable.

## Sequence
Day 0 — short problem/benefit email + personalized demo CTA.
Day 3 — one follow-up with one concrete use case.
Day 8 — proof/value follow-up.
Day 14 — final polite close-the-loop email.
Stop immediately on opt-out or negative reply.
