# Prospect personalization rules

Use this before every first-touch Europe outreach email.

1. Open the prospect's public website.
2. Extract only visible, relevant facts: services/products, city/service area, booking, opening hours, delivery/returns, or another obvious customer-facing detail.
3. Write one short personalization sentence. No fake compliments and no guessed problems.
4. Pick 2–3 customer questions that make sense for that exact company.
5. Insert those into `{{personalization}}` and `{{example_questions}}`.
6. Send only after the owner has said **"nyt"** and respondoai.fi has passed the final live check.

Good:
- “I noticed you offer home cleaning and window cleaning in Stockholm, so customers may have questions before booking.”
- “I noticed your store ships across Sweden and has several product categories, so visitors may want quick answers about delivery, returns and products.”

Bad:
- “I love your amazing company.”
- “You must be losing leads every day.”
- Any traffic, revenue, staff or customer-volume claim not visible on the site.

The goal is to make the email obviously written for the recipient while keeping it short.
