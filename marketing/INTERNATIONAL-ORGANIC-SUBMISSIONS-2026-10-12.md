## 2. Valmis englanninkielinen profiiliteksti

**Product name:** Respondo AI (Finland)

**Tagline:** Multilingual website customer service bot powered by your business knowledge

**Short description:**
Respondo AI is a Finland-based B2B website chatbot. It answers common customer questions using business-approved information, helps capture enquiries, supports booking workflows and hands uncertain questions to people. Finnish, Swedish and English are supported.

**Long description:**
Respondo AI helps businesses answer customer questions on their own websites using company-approved knowledge, rather than generic claims about services or pricing. Teams can add FAQs and import relevant information from their website. Visitors can ask about products, services, opening hours, deliveries, returns or appointments. Respondo AI can capture contact and booking requests and support staff taking over conversations when needed. The website, demo and documentation are available in Finnish, Swedish and English. The product is based in Finland. Paid business subscriptions are currently offered to companies in Finland only.

**Categories** (use only where appropriate): Website chatbot; customer support software; AI customer service; live chat / customer communication; knowledge base.

**Pricing:** Starter EUR 34.90/mo; Advanced EUR 44.90/mo; Business EUR 54.90/mo, with a three-day trial described on the site. Do not use an unverified conversion to USD.
