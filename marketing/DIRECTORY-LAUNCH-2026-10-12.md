## English tagline (Product Hunt or directory)

## Short English description
Respondo AI adds a multilingual customer-service chatbot to business websites. It answers with company-approved information, handles product and service questions, captures contact requests, and can hand conversations to a human.

## Longer English listing description
Respondo AI is a website customer-service chatbot designed for businesses. It uses information approved by each company to answer frequently asked questions about services, products, prices, opening hours and contact details. Businesses can maintain a question-and-answer knowledge base, import information from their website on eligible plans, capture contact requests, support booking workflows and let customer-service staff take over conversations when necessary. Conversations can be held in Finnish, Swedish and English. A three-day trial is available. Paid subscriptions are currently offered in Finland.
